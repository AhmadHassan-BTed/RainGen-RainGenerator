    What you see

    • Rings round the listener: for drops hitting hard ground and panels heard at ear height h, half the sound comes from within radius h and 90 % from within 3h.
    • Thunder wavefront: a sphere growing at the speed of sound for the air temperature, c = 331.3·√(1 + T/273.15) m/s. You hear the thunder the moment it reaches you: about 3 s per km.
    • Rain streaks: count follows the rate, fall speed the drop size (Atlas et al. 1973), slant the wind at ear height.
    • Badges: Auto follows the Listener macros, Offset keeps your adjustment on top, Pinned ignores macros, Anim follows a timeline track. Alt-click re-links.

    Flashes are limited to 3 per second; with “reduce motion” or Calm visuals they become a soft glow. Everything is synthesized from physics: no recordings, never loops.
